# Introducing Solid Foods

## BLW(baby-led weaning) method

Signs of readiness before beginning BLW

- Sit up independently with good head and neck control.
- Loss of tongue thrust reflex
- Brings toys or other objects to theirs mount
- Show interest in table foods

safe place to eat

always in a highchair. The parents should sit facing the baby

Understanding the difference of Gagging and Choking

gagging is a reflex tahat babys have o protect against choking. When gagging, a babys airway is not blocked and they are actvetily working to either swallow or spit out the piece of food
